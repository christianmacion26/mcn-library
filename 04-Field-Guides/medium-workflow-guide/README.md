# Medium Christian — Workflow Guide

> The production workflow used to write Medium posts for the *Christian
> Macion* publication. A field guide for self-published writers who want
> a small Claude-assisted workflow without losing their voice.

## What's in this guide

- The four-tool stack (Claude as drafter + judge + chart-maker + editor)
- The "first sentence = the claim" rule
- The post-visuals pattern: text + 1 chart + ≤ 2 inline visuals
- Pre-publish QA: factual review, citation review, voice review
- The publish-pack: how to bundle a post and its visuals for
  one-shot Medium upload
