The agent workflow for writing trading & quant posts — so you just proofread.

Voice: punchy / thread-style  ·  Audience: mixed general-finance  ·  Updated June 2026

Eight agents do the work; the main Claude session is the conductor that runs them in sequence and passes each one's output to the next. Subagents can't call each other, so the session orchestrates. You only step in at two moments: the angle (start) and the proofread (end).

The agents & what each does

AgentStageWhat it doesYour input that makes it shine
topic-scoutIdeate Generates & ranks post angles (a point of view, not just a topic) with hooks and a "why now". Appends keepers to ideas.md. A theme or a recent market event you care about; who it's for; anything you already believe about it.
researcherResearch Finds real, cited facts, data, and recent events; dates every number; fact-checks claims as confirmed / needs-nuance / unsupported. Escalates deep dives to the global deep-research skill. Saves briefs to research/. What you need to know or verify. It feeds the drafter real material so claims aren't invented.
post-drafterDraft Turns the chosen angle into a full first draft — titles, hook, subheaded sections, punchy close. Saves to drafts/. The locked angle + any data, anecdotes, or numbers you have. Target length if you have one.
post-editorEdit Ruthless line edit: stronger hook, cut 10–20% flab, fix pacing, gloss jargon, land the close. Preserves your voice. Usually nothing — it runs on the draft. Tell it if a section is sacred or if you want it shorter/punchier.
art-directorVisuals Designs the look: 2–3 cover concepts with ready-to-paste image-gen prompts (Midjourney / DALL·E / etc.), a palette, Medium specs, placement, captions + alt text. Can't generate AI art directly — it gives you the prompts. The draft or angle, and your image tool of choice. Any brand colors you like.
chart-makerVisuals Builds real chart/diagram PNGs (equity curves, distributions, schematics) as HTML/SVG rendered via Chrome — no Python needed. Saves to assets/ with caption + alt text. The data (CSV/numbers, or the S3 quant data). If it's illustrative, it's labeled as such.
post-exporterExport After you approve, renders the post to a Medium-styled PDF (so you see how it'll look) and appends a step-by-step guide to paste it into Medium — title/subtitle, cover placement, a markdown→editor formatting map, image captions + alt text, tags. Saves to exports/. Your sign-off. Drop the generated cover into assets/ first if you want it embedded.
post-producerAll 1–4 Self-contained version of the whole pipeline in one agent. Ideates → drafts → self-edits → fact-checks and returns one publish-ready post. A topic (or nothing). Best when you want minimal back-and-forth and trust it to pick the angle.

How you help — input that gets the best results

At the start (the angle)

  • Give a stance, not a subject. "Why retail backtests lie" beats "backtesting".
  • Drop any real numbers, trades, or charts you have — it kills the need to invent or flag data.
  • Name the reader if it's narrower than usual (e.g. "for prop-firm traders").
  • Say the length if you care (default 800–1,400 words).

At the end (the proofread)

  • Resolve any [VERIFY: …] flags — these are claims it couldn't confirm.
  • Check the hook and the last line first; they carry the post.
  • Confirm nothing reads as financial advice you don't want to make.
  • Say "ship it" / "export it" → you get a Medium-styled PDF + paste guide in exports/, and the file moves to published/.

One upgrade that changes everything: a voice sample

Drop one or two pieces of your own writing into the folder and tell the agents to match it. They'll mimic your cadence instead of a generic punchy style — the single highest-leverage thing you can do.

The non-negotiable rules (baked into every agent)

  • No fabricated data. Every stat/quote is sourced, flagged [VERIFY: …], or cut. Never invented.
  • Scroll-stopping hook in the first two lines — no throat-clearing.
  • Jargon gets a half-sentence inline gloss so the mixed audience never falls off.
  • Bold, specific claims backed by a number or mechanism — no hedging into mush.
  • Punchy close — the last line lands.
