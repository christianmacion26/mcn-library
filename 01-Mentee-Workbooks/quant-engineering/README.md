# Quant Research and Trading with a Tight-Context LLM — from the Ground Up

> A teaching workbook on **quant research and trading** workflows
> constrained to operate inside an LLM with a tight context window.
> The eval-gates pattern, the backtest discipline, the risk accounting.
> By Christian T. Macion.

This is the fourth workbook in the **Mentee Workbooks** series.

## What's in the workbook

| Part | Chapters | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| **A. The constraint** | 1 – 3 | What a tight context window does to a quant research pipeline; cite-don't-paste in financial data |
| **B. The gates** | 4 – 5 | G1–G31 in six families; rigorous null-model construction |
| **C. The trade** | 6 – 8 | Position sizing, drawdown path, regime-class overlays |
| **D. Reality** | 9 – 10 | Eight levers that compound + ten anti-patterns that compound the wrong way |
