# AI in Hedge Funds — Workbook

> A workbook-style research note on how AI/ML is being used inside
> institutional hedge funds, and what the audit-trail and risk-accounting
> expectations look like from the inside. By Christian T. Macion.

## What's in the workbook

- The institutional operating standard
- How ML/AI systems fit inside a CIO's risk stack
- The four common audit-trail gaps
- What "interpretability" actually has to mean for a senior review
- A worked walk-through: from a published research paper to a
  firm-routable signal
