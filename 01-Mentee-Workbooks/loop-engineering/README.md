# Loop-Engineering — from the Ground Up

> A teaching workbook on **self-improving agent loops**: how to design a
> loop that converges on outcomes instead of thrashing. By Christian T. Macion.

This is one of six teaching workbooks in the **Mentee Workbooks** series.
Built in the "from the Ground Up" format: cover, four parts, ten chapters,
hands-on examples. The same archetype used by the rest of the
[01-Mentee-Workbooks collection](../).

## What's in the workbook

| Part | Chapters | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| **A. The shape** | 1 – 3 | What a loop is, the four primitive operations (observe → model → act → evaluate), the loop-engineer skill pattern |
| **B. Designing**  | 4 – 5 | Convergence criteria, stop-conditions, multi-loop composition |
| **C. Operating**  | 6 – 8 | Boot, daily operation, postmortem patterns |
| **D. Optimizing** | 9 – 10 | Eight levers that compound + seven anti-patterns |
