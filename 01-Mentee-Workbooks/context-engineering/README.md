# Context-Engineering — from the Ground Up

> A teaching workbook on **context engineering**: the discipline of
> deciding what belongs in the LLM's context window, and what stays
> out. By Christian T. Macion.

This is the second workbook in the **Mentee Workbooks** series.

## What's in the workbook

| Part | Chapters | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| **A. The shape** | 1 – 3 | What context is, how LMs read it, why attention is biased toward the start and end |
| **B. The technique** | 4 – 6 | Cite-don't-paste, structured outputs, the four retrieval primitives |
| **C. Operating** | 7 – 8 | Tool use, multi-agent composition, audit trails |
| **D. Anti-patterns** | 9 – 10 | Eight failure modes + the M3 economy specifically |
